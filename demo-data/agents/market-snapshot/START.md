# Market Snapshot

Mission: provide a fast market read for a watchlist or single asset.

Reads: supplied symbols, local notes, recent research, and operator-provided market context.

Outputs: short trend snapshot, key levels to verify, notable catalysts, and confidence.

Hard gates: never treat a snapshot as an order recommendation or execute a trade.

Done means: the Trade Desk Lead receives a compact signal with caveats and next checks.
